About this platform

This platform brings together a growing collection of interactive, dynamic virtual laboratories that support the teaching and learning of feedback control systems. Each lab is a single self-contained page, so it can be used in class, assigned as homework, or opened offline.

The laboratories were developed with the assistance of generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) tools. These laboratories were done using Claude Sonnet 5.5, made by Anthropic, following simple prompts provided by Paulo Moura Oliveira. The PID Explorer Lab was done by Damir Vrancic. As with any teaching material, instructors and students are encouraged to check results against the theory and to report anything that looks wrong.
