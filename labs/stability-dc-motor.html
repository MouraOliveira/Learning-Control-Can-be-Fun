Stability of a DC‑motor position control loop

A motor drives a load. Its angle θ is measured and compared with the reference θref; the controller is a pure gain K (proportional control).

Model

Plant (normalised): G(s) = K / [ s (s+1)(s+2) ] — an integrator (voltage → speed → angle), one pole at s = −1 from the mechanical time constant (τm = 1 s) and one at s = −2 from the armature electrical time constant (τe = 0.5 s).

Closed loop (unity feedback): T(s) = K / (s³ + 3s² + 2s + K). Poles = roots of the characteristic equation s³ + 3s² + 2s + K = 0.
