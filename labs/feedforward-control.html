Feedforward Control Lab

Output-disturbance compensation: feedforward (FF) alone vs. feedback (FB) alone vs. FB + FF. Pick a scenario, then tweak anything.

    Theory. Process: y = Gp·u + Gd·d, with Gp = Kp e−θp s/(τp s+1) and Gd = Kd e−θd s/(τd s+1). Perfect cancellation needs u = −(Gd/Gp)·d, i.e. Gff = (Kd/Kp)·(τp s+1)/(τd s+1)·e−(θd−θp)s. It is realizable only if θd ≥ θp (plus any measurement delay) and the actuator can supply the required lead. Static FF keeps only the gain Kd/Kp. FB (PI) corrects whatever FF leaves behind but only after the error appears.
